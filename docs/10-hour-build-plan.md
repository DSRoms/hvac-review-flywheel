# HVAC Review Flywheel — 10-Hour Build Plan

## Goal
Build a solo-run, productized reputation system for HVAC and other home-service businesses.

## Offer
**Review Flywheel**: done-for-you review capture + response system that asks every happy customer, replies to reviews, flags bad ones, and reports monthly results.

## Target buyer
HVAC owners with 5–25 techs who depend on Google Maps, referrals, and reputation to close jobs.

## Pricing
- Setup: $750–$1,500
- Monthly: $300–$800
- Optional add-on: $150/location

## 10-Hour Schedule
### Hour 1
Lock niche, promise, pricing, and service boundaries.

### Hour 2
Write the one-page site copy and CTA.

### Hour 3
Create the intake form questions and onboarding checklist.

### Hour 4
Write the service SOP: audit, install, request, response, reporting.

### Hour 5
Build the first sample report and before/after mockup.

### Hour 6
Create a lead list template and outreach tracker.

### Hour 7
Collect 25 HVAC prospects in one metro.

### Hour 8
Write two outreach variants and a follow-up message.

### Hour 9
Package everything into a simple sales folder.

### Hour 10
Send the first outreach batch and review replies.

## Minimum viable launch assets
- one-page landing page
- intake checklist
- sample report
- lead list template
- outreach script
- fulfillment SOP
