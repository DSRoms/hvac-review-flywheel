# Prospecting Workflow

1. Pick one metro.
2. Search Google Maps for HVAC companies.
3. Filter for businesses with:
   - weak review velocity
   - slow owner replies
   - decent service quality but mediocre reputation
4. Fill 25 rows in the lead sheet.
5. Send 5 personalized messages first.
6. Use the audit promise to start conversations.
7. Follow up 2 days later.

## First search terms
- HVAC near me
- air conditioning repair
- furnace repair
- heating and cooling
- AC service
